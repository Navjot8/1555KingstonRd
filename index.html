1555 Kingston Rd #421, Pickering, ON

Welcome to 1555 Kingston Rd #421, a bright, stylish Marshall Homes townhome in the heart of Pickering. With approx. 1,400 sq. ft. of open-concept living and a private 400 sq. ft. rooftop terrace, this home is made for everyday comfort and effortless entertaining.

2 Beds • 3 Baths • ~1,400 Sq. Ft. • 400 Sq. Ft. Terrace • 2 Parking Spaces

Residence Highlights

Bedrooms & Bathrooms: 2 bedrooms | 3 bathrooms configured for modern lifestyle convenience.
Spacious Layout: Approx. 1,400 sq. ft. of interior living space plus an approx. 400 sq. ft. private rooftop terrace.
Natural Light: Loads of natural sunlight pour into the living area and bedrooms, keeping the home warm and cheerful.
Living & Dining: Open-concept living and dining areas designed for effortless entertaining.
Gourmet Kitchen: Large kitchen equipped with quartz counters, centre island, and stylish tile backsplash.
Primary Suite: Features a walk-in closet and private 4-piece ensuite bathroom.
Laundry Convenience: Dedicated ensuite laundry facilities.
Parking: Includes 2 designated parking spaces (underground + surface).
Community: Pet-friendly community situated in the heart of Pickering.
Prime Location: Steps to GO transit, Hwy 401, public transit, Pickering Town Centre, parks, and schools.
